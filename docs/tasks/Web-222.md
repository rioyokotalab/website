# Web-222 — Equal producer and consumer permissions

Producer and consumer DRIVERs are trust-equivalent repository agents. Consumer
DRIVERs should normally avoid producer queue files and new goal allocation to
reduce conflicts, but may perform either when they judge it necessary for an
in-scope solution. They fetch first, avoid known overlap, update ledger
invariants atomically, record the reason, and use the same protected workflow.

Role-aware diff validation becomes advisory for ownership and retains immutable
packet protection. Deployment, credential, public-content, language-parity,
and external-action rules remain unchanged and apply equally to producer and
consumer DRIVERs. The separate WORKER restrictions remain because a delegated
worker has narrower dispatch authority and is not the repository consumer role.

Owner consent requires no magic phrase. Clear contextual approval of the latest
bounded proposal is sufficient; agents do not ask the owner to paste their
authorization text. This task does not deploy the site.

## Outcome

Producer and consumer DRIVERs now share repository capability while deployment,
credential, public-content, language-parity, external-action, and delegated
WORKER boundaries remain unchanged. Role overlap is advisory and immutable
packets remain hard for both DRIVER roles.
